
## Crop

Common Name: Wheat
Scientific Name: Triticum aestivum
Crop Category: Cereal
Primary Uses: Food grain, flour, animal feed

## Major Growth Stages

1. Germination
2. Crown Root Initiation
3. Tillering
4. Stem Extension
5. Flowering/Heading
6. Grain Filling
7. Maturity

## Critical Growth Stages

Water availability is particularly important during:
- Crown Root Initiation
- Flowering/heading
- Grain filling

Water stress during critical stages can negatively affect yield.

## Water Management

Important factors:
- Soil moisture
- Rainfall
- Temperature
- Evapotranspiration
- Crop growth stage
- Soil type
- Previous irrigation

AgriShield should avoid fixed irrigation schedules and calculate irrigation requirements using current field conditions.

## Water Stress Indicators

Potential indicators:
- Low soil moisture
- High temperature
- Low rainfall forecast
- Increasing evapotranspiration
- Declining vegetation index
- Visible leaf stress

## Heat Stress

High temperatures during flowering and grain filling can reduce grain development and yield.

Risk increases when:
- Temperature is significantly above the crop's optimal range
- Soil moisture is low
- Atmospheric demand is high

## Disease Risk

Potential diseases include:
- Wheat rust
- Powdery mildew
- Fusarium-related diseases

Disease risk should be evaluated using:
- Temperature
- Humidity
- Crop growth stage
- Historical disease occurrence
- Field observations
