
## Crop

Common Name: Cotton
Scientific Name: Gossypium spp.
Category: Fibre Cash Crop

## Growth Stages

1. Germination
2. Vegetative growth
3. Squaring
4. Flowering
5. Boll development
6. Boll opening
7. Harvest

## Water Management

Water requirement depends on:
- Variety
- Soil
- Climate
- Crop stage
- Rainfall
- Evapotranspiration

Water stress during flowering and boll development can affect yield.

## Major Risks

- Drought
- Heat stress
- Excess rainfall
- Boll damage
- Pest infestation

## Pest Risk

Important pests may include:
- Bollworms
- Whiteflies
- Aphids
- Thrips

## AI Monitoring

AgriShield should combine:
- Weather
- Crop stage
- Satellite vegetation indices
- Field observations
- Historical pest information

to identify high-risk zones.

## Recommended AI Actions

- Inspect high-risk zones
- Optimize irrigation
- Avoid unnecessary pesticide application
- Monitor crop development
- Estimate yield risk
