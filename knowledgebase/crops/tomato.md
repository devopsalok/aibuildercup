
## Crop

Common Name: Tomato
Scientific Name: Solanum lycopersicum
Category: Vegetable / High-Value Crop

## Growth Stages

1. Seedling
2. Vegetative growth
3. Flowering
4. Fruit setting
5. Fruit development
6. Ripening

## Major Risks

- Heat stress
- Water stress
- Excess moisture
- Fungal disease
- Bacterial disease
- Insect infestation

## Disease Risk

Disease risk can increase with favorable combinations of:
- Humidity
- Temperature
- Leaf wetness
- Crop stage

## AI Recommendation

The system should identify high-risk zones and recommend inspection before blanket treatment.

## Sustainability

Targeted intervention can reduce unnecessary pesticide and water use.
