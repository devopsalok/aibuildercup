

## Crop

Common Name: Soybean
Scientific Name: Glycine max
Category: Oilseed / Commercial Crop

## Growth Stages

1. Germination
2. Vegetative growth
3. Flowering
4. Pod development
5. Seed filling
6. Maturity

## Critical Conditions

Water stress around flowering and pod/seed development can reduce yield.

## Risks

- Drought
- Excess rainfall
- Waterlogging
- Disease
- Insect pressure

## AI Inputs

- Soil moisture
- Temperature
- Rainfall
- Humidity
- NDVI
- Crop stage
- Historical disease/pest occurrence

## AI Outputs

- Stress probability
- Disease risk
- Pest risk
- Yield forecast
- Recommended intervention
