
## Crop

Common Name: Rice
Scientific Name: Oryza sativa
Crop Category: Cereal
Primary Uses: Food grain

## Major Growth Stages

1. Germination
2. Seedling establishment
3. Tillering
4. Panicle initiation
5. Flowering
6. Grain filling
7. Maturity

## Water Management

Rice has relatively high water requirements compared with many crops.

Important factors:
- Soil type
- Rainfall
- Temperature
- Crop establishment method
- Irrigation method
- Growth stage

AgriShield should optimize water availability rather than assume continuous flooding is always required.

## Water Stress

Potential indicators:
- Declining soil moisture
- Reduced vegetation index
- High temperature
- Insufficient rainfall
- Crop-stage-specific water deficit

## Excess Water Risk

Excessive water or poor drainage can create:
- Waterlogging
- Root-zone problems
- Nutrient loss
- Disease-favorable conditions

AgriShield should therefore evaluate both:
- Water deficit risk
- Excess-water risk

## Climate Risks

Important risks:
- Heat stress
- Drought
- Flooding
- Heavy rainfall
- Cyclonic weather

## Disease/Pest Risk

Potential problems include:
- Rice blast
- Bacterial diseases
- Stem borers
- Planthoppers

Risk assessment should combine crop stage, weather and field observations.

## AI Decision

AgriShield should determine:
- Irrigation requirement
- Waterlogging risk
- Heat risk
- Disease risk
- Expected yield risk

## Sustainability

The optimization objective should include:
- Reduced unnecessary irrigation
- Reduced fertilizer losses
- Reduced pesticide use
- Maintained or improved yield
