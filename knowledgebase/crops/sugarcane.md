
## Crop

Common Name: Sugarcane
Scientific Name: Saccharum spp.
Category: Commercial Cash Crop

## Growth Stages

1. Germination/establishment
2. Tillering
3. Grand growth
4. Maturation

## Resource Requirements

Sugarcane generally has substantial water requirements.

AgriShield should monitor:
- Soil moisture
- Rainfall
- Evapotranspiration
- Temperature
- Crop age

## Major Risks

- Water stress
- Excess water
- Heat
- Pest infestation
- Disease
- Lodging

## Pest/Disease Monitoring

Potential threats include:
- Borers
- White grubs
- Aphids
- Red rot
- Smut

## AI Outputs

- Water requirement
- Stress risk
- Pest/disease risk
- Yield estimate
- Irrigation recommendation

## Sustainability Objective

Optimize irrigation and nutrient management while maintaining expected yield.
