# Soil Intelligence

## Purpose

Evaluate how soil characteristics influence crop health,
water availability and nutrient management.

## Key Parameters

- Soil type
- Soil moisture
- Soil pH
- Nitrogen
- Phosphorus
- Potassium
- Organic matter
- Electrical conductivity
- Soil temperature
- Water-holding capacity

## Soil Moisture

Soil moisture should be interpreted together with:

- Crop type
- Root depth
- Growth stage
- Soil texture
- Weather
- Evapotranspiration

A single soil-moisture value should not automatically trigger
an irrigation recommendation.

## Soil pH

Soil pH affects nutrient availability and crop suitability.

Interpretation should be crop-specific.

## Soil Nutrients

Nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium requirements depend on:

- Crop
- Growth stage
- Existing soil nutrients
- Expected yield
- Previous fertilizer application

## AI Principle

AgriShield should avoid generic fertilizer recommendations.

Recommendations should consider soil test results and authoritative
local agronomic guidance.

## Output

Soil Health Score:
0–100

Water Availability:
Low / Medium / High

Nutrient Risk:
Low / Medium / High
