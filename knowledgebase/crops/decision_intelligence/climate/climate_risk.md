# Climate Risk Intelligence

## Objective

Predict agricultural risks caused by abnormal weather and climate events.

## Risk Categories

- Drought
- Heatwave
- Excess rainfall
- Flooding
- Waterlogging
- Cold stress
- Unseasonal rainfall
- Strong winds

## Inputs

- Current weather
- Weather forecast
- Historical climate
- Crop growth stage
- Soil moisture
- Soil type
- Crop sensitivity

## Climate Risk Score

Climate Risk =
Hazard Probability
×
Crop Vulnerability
×
Exposure

## Output

Hazard:
Probability:
Expected Duration:
Affected Area:
Crop Vulnerability:
Expected Impact:
Recommended Preparation:

## Principle

Early warning should trigger preparedness rather than automatic intervention.
