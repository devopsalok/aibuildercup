# Farm Economics Intelligence

## Objective

Evaluate the economic impact of agricultural decisions.

## Cost Components

- Seeds
- Fertilizer
- Pesticide
- Water
- Electricity/fuel
- Labor
- Machinery
- Harvesting
- Transportation

## Revenue

Expected Revenue =
Expected Yield × Expected Market Price

## Profit

Estimated Profit =
Expected Revenue - Estimated Production Cost

## Decision Analysis

For each possible intervention calculate:

- Additional cost
- Expected yield impact
- Expected revenue impact
- Resource consumption
- Expected profit change

## What-if Examples

Scenario A:
Irrigate now

Scenario B:
Delay irrigation

Scenario C:
Use reduced irrigation

The system should compare expected outcomes.

## Important Principle

Market prices and cost assumptions must be timestamped
and region-specific when available.
