## Data Sources

- NDVI
- NDWI
- EVI
- Soil moisture
- Temperature
- Rainfall
- Humidity
- Crop stage
- Field observations

## Temporal Principle

A single observation should not automatically indicate crop stress.

AgriShield should evaluate:

Current condition
+
Historical trend
+
Expected future condition
