# Crop Disease Risk Intelligence

## Objective

Predict environmental conditions that may increase disease risk.

## Inputs

- Crop
- Variety where available
- Growth stage
- Temperature
- Relative humidity
- Rainfall
- Leaf wetness where available
- Soil moisture
- Historical disease occurrence
- Satellite/field imagery

## Disease Risk Model

Disease risk can be represented as:

Disease Risk =
Environmental Favorability
+
Crop Susceptibility
+
Historical Risk
+
Observed Symptoms

## Risk Levels

LOW:
No immediate concern.

MEDIUM:
Increase monitoring.

HIGH:
Inspect affected zones and follow validated crop-protection guidance.

CRITICAL:
Immediate field assessment required.

## AI Principle

Environmental risk prediction does not confirm disease.

Field symptoms or validated diagnostic methods should be used
before treatment decisions.
