

## Crop

Common Name: Maize
Scientific Name: Zea mays
Crop Category: Cereal
Uses: Food, feed, industrial raw material

## Growth Stages

1. Germination
2. Vegetative development
3. Rapid vegetative growth
4. Tasseling
5. Silking
6. Grain filling
7. Maturity

## Critical Stages

Water and temperature stress around:
- Tasseling
- Silking
- Grain filling

can have significant effects on yield.

## Water Management

AgriShield should use:
- Soil moisture
- Rainfall forecast
- Temperature
- Evapotranspiration
- Crop stage
- Soil type

to estimate water stress.

## Heat Stress

High temperature during flowering and pollination can negatively affect reproductive success.

## Pest Risk

Potential pests include:
- Fall armyworm
- Stem borers
- Corn earworm

Risk models should use:
- Temperature
- Humidity
- Crop stage
- Historical pest occurrence
- Field observations

## Disease Risk

Potential diseases:
- Maize leaf blight
- Rust
- Ear-related fungal diseases

## AI Outputs

- Water stress probability
- Heat stress probability
- Pest risk
- Disease risk
- Yield forecast
- Irrigation recommendation

## Sustainability

Prefer targeted interventions over blanket field treatment.
