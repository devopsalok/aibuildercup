
## Crop

Common Name: Chilli / Chili Pepper
Scientific Name: Capsicum annuum and related species
Category: Spice / Cash Crop

## Growth Stages

1. Nursery/seedling
2. Vegetative growth
3. Flowering
4. Fruit development
5. Maturity/harvest

## Major Risks

- Water stress
- Excess moisture
- Heat stress
- Thrips
- Aphids
- Mites
- Fungal and bacterial diseases

## AI Monitoring

AgriShield should evaluate:
- Temperature
- Humidity
- Soil moisture
- Rainfall
- Crop stage
- Field imagery

## Recommendation

Provide early warnings and prioritize inspection of high-risk zones.

## Sustainability

Use targeted crop-protection actions instead of blanket pesticide application where agronomically appropriate.
