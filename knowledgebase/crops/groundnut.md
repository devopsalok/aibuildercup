
## Crop

Common Name: Groundnut / Peanut
Scientific Name: Arachis hypogaea
Category: Oilseed / Cash Crop

## Growth Stages

1. Germination
2. Vegetative growth
3. Flowering
4. Pegging
5. Pod formation
6. Pod filling
7. Maturity

## Important Risk Periods

Water stress during flowering, pegging and pod development can affect production.

## Major Risks

- Drought
- Excess moisture
- Fungal disease
- Insect infestation

## AI Monitoring

Use:
- Soil moisture
- Rainfall
- Humidity
- Temperature
- Crop stage
- Vegetation indices
