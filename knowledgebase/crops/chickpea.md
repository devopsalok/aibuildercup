
## Crop

Common Name: Chickpea / Gram
Scientific Name: Cicer arietinum
Category: Pulse

## Growth Stages

1. Germination
2. Vegetative growth
3. Branching
4. Flowering
5. Pod formation
6. Seed filling
7. Maturity

## Major Risks

- Drought
- Excess rainfall
- Temperature stress
- Pod damage
- Fungal disease
- Insect infestation
